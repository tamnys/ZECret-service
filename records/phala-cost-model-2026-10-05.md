The new optional rate-basis journal records that split per tracked CVM without changing its original rate, identity, deadline or earlier floor. A tracked DELETE must have a retained 204 outcome; the original manually deleted CVM instead requires an explicit operator-asserted manual-deletion basis and an earlier authenticated present observation. Both paths also require a later completed workspace inventory in which the CVM is absent and its authenticated detail returns 404. Compute is modeled at the full combined rate through that *later observation time*, then storage continues at the reviewed rate. Reappearance restores the full modeled rate. Unrated, merely stopped, uncertain, or incompletely observed resources continue at the original full rate. The prior 38,832,817 micro-USD floor never decreases.

This provisional cost bound does not declare storage deletion, billing reconciliation, cleanup completion, or permission to exceed the original ceiling. Before another CVM, the operator must inspect the original ledger at the current clock, confirm the rate-basis journal and provider inventory, and fit the new run plus teardown allowance beneath the unchanged trigger and ceiling.
